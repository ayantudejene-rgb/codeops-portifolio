Exercise 5: Inspect an Ethiopian Website

Open an Ethiopian website in a browser, press F12 (or right-click and choose Inspect), then use the Elements panel to identify its header, nav, and footer.

Inspection Notes

  • Header: The top area containing the site's branding or introductory content.
  • Nav: The navigation area containing links to important pages.
  • Footer: The bottom area containing information such as contact details, links, or copyright.

The exercise requires inspection of a real Ethiopian website in the browser, so these notes record what to look for rather than pretending an inspection was performed here.
